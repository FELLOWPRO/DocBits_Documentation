# Compare with Quote Price

The cards in this category, shown in the **Add Card** library of the Workflow Builder:

The cards in this category compare the line items of a document with the supplier item price data that DocBits found for the document's supplier and its supplier item IDs. Depending on the card, this can be the supplier item ID, the item number, the managed price or the last price paid, the unit of measure or the supplier type. Version 3 of the Unit of Measure card can also compare with the lines of the matched purchase order.
