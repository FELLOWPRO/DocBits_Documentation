# Partner cards: custom extensions

**Partner cards are custom workflow cards created by customers or partners using the Card SDK.** Their functions, inputs and versions depend on the installed package; availability can differ between organizations.

They can appear in the partner-card category under **And** or **Then**. Individual customer and partner cards are outside the standard-card reference on these pages. Refer to the card creator’s documentation for the specific function, configuration and examples.

Cards with the same name, such as **My Action**, can have different implementations. Do not assume they perform the same action or accept the same settings.

See [Card SDK](../card-sdk.md) for creating and managing custom cards.
