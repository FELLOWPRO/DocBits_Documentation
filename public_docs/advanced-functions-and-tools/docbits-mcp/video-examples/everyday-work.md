# Everyday work with Claude

Spend less time finding the document, searching its history, and asking someone what went wrong. Ask Claude to bring the available evidence together.

## Try this question

> Why is this invoice waiting? Show me the relevant fields and log messages, explain the blocker, and suggest the next action.

Claude can inspect the document information available through your connection and explain the recorded validation problem. You get a practical next step with the evidence behind it.

Follow up with:

> Compare the affected fields with the original document. What should I check before correcting it?
