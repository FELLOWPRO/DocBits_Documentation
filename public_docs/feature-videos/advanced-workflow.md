Build document workflows with connected cards. Route by conditions, join results and give errors their own path — with the complete process in view.

## From invoice to the next action

An incoming invoice starts the example. A total amount greater than 1,000 takes the Approval route; 1,000 or less takes Standard. The film then shows genuine test runs with 1,500 and 500 in the DocBits designer, including their results.

## Three ways to control the flow

| Feature | In practice |
| --- | --- |
| **Choose the route** | Connect Start or When to a condition. Success, Failed Condition and Error have distinct outputs. |
| **Join connected results** | Wait ANY requires at least one matching connected source. Wait ALL requires a matching connected outcome from every source. Neither cancels other actions. |
| **Connect the next action** | Use error paths, Call API and notes to connect systems and explain the design. Notes do not execute actions. |
