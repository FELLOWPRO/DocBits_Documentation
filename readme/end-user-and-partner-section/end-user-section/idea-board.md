# Idea Board

The Idea Board is the place to submit feature requests, report missing functionality, and vote on what the product team should build next. Ideas are visible to everyone in your organisation, with comments and voting.

## How to access

Open the sidebar and click **Ideas**, or navigate to `/ideas` directly.

## Browsing ideas

Each idea is shown as a card with:

* The title and a rich-text description (which can include images).
* A vote button with the current count.
* A category badge — Feature, Improvement, Bug, Integration, or Other.
* A status chip — New, Under Review, Planned, In Progress, Done, or Declined.
* The submitter's name and organisation, and a relative timestamp.
* A comments toggle that expands the discussion below the card.

Pagination at the bottom of the list moves between pages.

## Submitting a new idea

Click **New idea** in the header. The dialog asks for:

* **Title** — a short, descriptive name for the idea.
* **Description** — a rich-text editor with bold, italic, underline, strike-through, lists, and links. You can paste or upload images directly into the description.
* **Category** — pick the closest match. The product team uses this to route ideas.

Press **Submit** to publish. The idea appears in the list with the **New** status.

## Voting

Click the vote button on any card to add your vote. Click again to remove it. Vote totals drive the **Most Voted** sort and signal demand to the product team.

## Commenting

Open an idea's comment thread to ask questions, add context, or share workarounds. You can edit and delete your own comments. The submitter and admins can also delete comments to keep threads on-topic.

## Filtering and sorting

The header above the list provides:

* A search box matching against title and description.
* A category dropdown.
* A status dropdown.
* A sort selector — **Newest** or **Most Voted**.

Filters combine, so you can narrow to "Most Voted Improvements that are Under Review" in three clicks.

## Editing or deleting your own idea

The card menu (three-dot button) lets you edit or delete an idea you submitted. Once the product team has changed an idea's status away from **New**, editing the title or category is locked — comment on the idea instead.

<mark>The Idea Board is for product feedback, not for incident reports. Use a support ticket for issues that are blocking your work today.</mark>
