# Tasks

This is where you can keep an eye on your tasks. You can pick between two different layouts – one is a bit more detailed than the other. You can also filter tasks by different criteria, like whether they're assigned to you or the status of the task.

### When you cannot mark a task as done

Your organisation can limit who may close tasks, that is, set them to **Done** or **Rejected**. If it does, the **Done** button, the **Done** and **Rejected** statuses and **Mark as done** are not offered for tasks you may not close, and the task board does not let you drop such a task into the **Done** or **Rejected** column. You can still edit the task, save it and move it between **Open** and **In progress**.

An admin of your organisation chooses who may close tasks. See [Tasks & Notifications](../../administration-and-setup/settings/document-processing/module/tasks-and-notifications.md#non-admin-task-completion).
