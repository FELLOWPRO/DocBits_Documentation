# Videos & Tutorials

**Videos & Tutorials** plays short DocBits videos without leaving the page you are working on. The videos are grouped by topic, so you can find the one that matches the task in front of you.

## Open the panel

The video icon sits at the right end of the top bar, after the help icon.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/videos-tutorials-header.png" alt="The DocBits top bar with the yellow Videos and Tutorials icon as the last icon on the right"><figcaption><p>The video icon is the last icon in the top bar.</p></figcaption></figure>

* The icon is **yellow with a small dot** while at least one video has not been acknowledged.
* It takes the same colour as the other header icons once you have acknowledged every video.

Select the icon to open **Videos & Tutorials** as a panel on the right. The panel stays open while you move through DocBits, so you can watch a step and repeat it in the page behind it.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/videos-tutorials-panel.png" alt="The Videos and Tutorials panel listing the topics Document processing, Workflows, PO Matching, E-Documents, Validation and rules, Analytics and Administration as folders with video counts"><figcaption><p>The panel opens on the list of topics. Each topic is a folder with its number of videos.</p></figcaption></figure>

## Browse a topic

The panel opens on the **topic folders**, for example **Document processing**, **Workflows**, **E-Documents** or **Administration**. A folder shows how many videos it contains and a **NEW** marker while it holds a video you have not acknowledged.

Select a folder to open it. Inside you find the videos of that topic and, where the topic is large, further folders that group them, such as **Countries** under **E-Documents**. A breadcrumb at the top and the back arrow take you up one level. A video you opened from a folder returns to that folder when you close it.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/videos-tutorials-folders.png" alt="The E-Documents folder open in the Videos and Tutorials panel, with the breadcrumb, two videos and the Countries subfolder holding 40 videos"><figcaption><p>Open a topic to see its videos and its subfolders.</p></figcaption></figure>

## Search for a video

Type into **Search videos** to look through every topic at once. Each result names the folder it belongs to, for example **Workflows › Building workflows**, so you know where the video sits. The search also matches topic and folder names and ignores capitals and accents.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/videos-tutorials-search.png" alt="Search results for invoice in the Videos and Tutorials panel, each video showing its topic and group below the title"><figcaption><p>The search covers all topics and shows the folder of every result.</p></figcaption></figure>

Clear the search field to return to the topic folders.

## Watch a video and acknowledge it

Select a video to play it in the panel. The title and a short description say what the video covers.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/videos-tutorials-player.png" alt="A video playing in the Videos and Tutorials panel with its title, description and the Acknowledge button"><figcaption><p>Play the video in the panel, then acknowledge it.</p></figcaption></figure>

Select **Acknowledge** when you have watched the video. This marks it as seen for your user only: the **NEW** marker on the video and on its folder disappears for you, and the icon in the top bar turns grey once nothing is left unacknowledged. Other users still see their own unacknowledged videos.

## Videos for administrators and for users

* Videos marked **Admin** are meant for administrators. Administrators see both administrator and end-user videos in the panel; end users see the end-user videos only.
* If a video is not available in your interface language, the English version is shown with a language code such as **EN**.
* The catalogue is read when you open the panel. If no videos are available, the icon keeps its normal colour and the panel shows no videos.
